1 答案 E

模 \(x^2-2\) 时，\(x^2\equiv2\)，所以 \(x^3-2\equiv2x-2\)。这个一次多项式就是余式。

所以正确答案是 E。

English

Modulo \(x^2-2,\) we have \(x^2\equiv2,\) so \(x^3-2\equiv2x-2.\) This linear polynomial is the remainder.

Therefore, the correct answer is E.

2 答案 A

\(x\) 的八倍增加二后是 \(8x+2\)。它的四分之一为 \(\frac{8x+2}{4}=2x+\frac12\)。

所以正确答案是 A。

English

Eight times \(x,\) increased by two, is \(8x+2.\) One fourth is \(\frac{8x+2}{4}=2x+\frac12.\)

Therefore, the correct answer is A.

3 答案 C

当 \(x=2\) 时，\(x^x=2^2=4\)。因此 \((x^x)^{(x^x)}=4^4=256\)。

所以正确答案是 C。

English

At \(x=2,\) \(x^x=2^2=4.\) Hence \((x^x)^{(x^x)}=4^4=256.\)

Therefore, the correct answer is C.

4 答案 E

当 \(r\gt0\) 时，周长与面积相等给出 \(\pi r+2r=\frac{\pi r^2}{2}\)。两边除以 \(r\) 并求解，得到 \(r=\frac{2(\pi+2)}{\pi}=2+\frac{4}{\pi}\)。

所以正确答案是 E。

English

For \(r\gt0,\) equality of perimeter and area gives \(\pi r+2r=\frac{\pi r^2}{2}.\) Dividing by \(r\) and solving yields \(r=\frac{2(\pi+2)}{\pi}=2+\frac{4}{\pi}.\)

Therefore, the correct answer is E.

5 答案 C

写成 \(x=ka\) 和 \(y=kb\)。因为 \(a\lt b\)，所以 \(x\) 较小。由 \(k(a+b)=c\) 得 \(k=\frac{c}{a+b}\)，因此 \(x=\frac{ac}{a+b}\)。

所以正确答案是 C。

English

Write \(x=ka\) and \(y=kb.\) Since \(a\lt b,\) \(x\) is smaller. From \(k(a+b)=c,\) \(k=\frac{c}{a+b},\) so \(x=\frac{ac}{a+b}.\)
