1单选

Theresa 的父母同意，如果她平均每周花 \(10\) 小时帮忙做家务，连续 \(6\) 周，就给她买票去看她最喜欢的乐队。前 \(5\) 周她分别帮忙 \(8\)、\(11\)、\(7\)、\(12\) 和 \(10\) 小时。为了得到门票，最后一周她必须工作多少小时？

English（选项同上方）

Theresa’s parents have agreed to buy her tickets to see her favorite band if she spends an average of \(10\) hours per week helping around the house for \(6\) weeks. For the first \(5\) weeks she helps around the house for \(8,\) \(11,\) \(7,\) \(12\) and \(10\) hours. How many hours must she work for the final week to earn the tickets?

前 \(5\) 周 Theresa 总共工作 \[8+11+7+12+10=48\] 小时。

她承诺总共工作 \(10\cdot 6=60\) 小时。

因此她最后一周必须工作 \(60-48=12\) 小时才能得到门票。

所以正确答案是 D。

English

During the first \(5\) weeks, Theresa works for a total of \[8+11+7+12+10=48\] hours.

She promised to work \(10\cdot 6=60\) hours in all.

This means that she has to work \(60-48=12\) hours during the final week to earn the tickets.

Thus, D is the correct answer.

2单选

对 \(650\) 名学生进行了意大利面偏好调查。选项包括千层面、意式夹馅卷、意式馄饨和意大利面条。调查结果显示在条形图中。喜欢意大利面条的学生人数与喜欢意式夹馅卷的学生人数之比是多少？

English（选项同上方）

\(650\) students were surveyed about their pasta preferences. The choices were lasagna, manicotti, ravioli and spaghetti. The results of the survey are displayed in the bar graph. What is the ratio of the number of students who preferred spaghetti to the number of students who preferred manicotti?

喜欢意大利面条的学生有 \(250\) 人，喜欢意式夹馅卷的学生有 \(100\) 人。

比值为 \[ \dfrac{250}{100} = \dfrac{5}{2}\text{。}\]

所以正确答案是 E。

English

There are \(250\) students who preferred spaghetti and \(100\) that preferred manicotti.

The ratio is therefore \[ \dfrac{250}{100} = \dfrac{5}{2}. \]

Thus, E is the correct answer.

3单选

\(250\) 的两个最小质因数之和是多少？

English（选项同上方）

What is the sum of the two smallest prime factors of \(250?\)

对 \(250\) 分解质因数：\[ 250 = 2 \cdot 5^3\text{。}\]因此 \(250\) 只有两个质因数：\(2\) 和 \(5\)。它们的和为 \(7\)。

所以正确答案是 C。

English

We can prime factorize \(250\) to get \[ 250 = 2 \cdot 5^3. \] From this, we can see that \(250\) only has two prime factors: \(2\) and \(5.\) The sum of these is \(7.\)

Thus, C is the correct answer.

4单选

一座鬼屋有六扇窗。幽灵 Georgie 从一扇窗进入，并从另一扇不同的窗离开，共有多少种方式？

English（选项同上方）

A haunted house has six windows. In how many ways can Georgie the Ghost enter the house by one window and leave by a different window?

Georgie 有 \(6\) 种选择进入的窗户。但离开时只能选择不同的窗户，所以有 \(5\) 种选择。

路径总数为 \(6 \cdot 5 = 30\)。

所以正确答案是 D。

English

Georgie has \(6\) options for which window he enters through. He, however, only has \(5\) options for the exit since it must be different from the entrance.

The total number of paths is therefore \(6 \cdot 5 = 30.\)

Thus, D is the correct answer.

5单选

Chandler 想买一辆 \(\$500\) 的山地车。生日时，他的祖父母给他 \(\$50\)，姑姑给他 \(\$35\)，表亲给他 \(\$15\)。他送报每周赚 \(\$16\)。他会使用所有生日钱和所有送报赚的钱。多少周后他可以买这辆山地车？

English（选项同上方）

Chandler wants to buy a \(\$500\) mountain bike. For his birthday, his grandparents send him \(\$50\), his aunt sends him \(\$35\) and his cousin gives him \(\$15\). He earns \(\$16\) per week for his paper route. He will use all of his birthday money and all of the money he earns from his paper route. In how many weeks will he be able to buy the mountain bike?

Chandler 的生日钱总额为 \(50+35+15=100\) 美元。因此他还需要 \(500-100=400\) 美元。

送报需要 \(400\div 16=25\) 周赚到这笔钱。

所以正确答案是 B。

English

The total amount of birthday money Chandler gets is \(50+35+15=100\) dollars. Therefore, he still needs \(500-100=400\) dollars.

This can be earned in \(400\div 16=25\) weeks through his paper route.

Thus, B is the correct answer.

6单选

\(1985\) 年，美国长途电话平均费用为每分钟 \(41\) 美分；\(2005\) 年为每分钟 \(7\) 美分。求长途电话每分钟费用大约下降了百分之多少。

English（选项同上方）

The average cost of a long-distance call in the USA in \(1985\) was \(41\) cents per minute, and the average cost of a long-distance call in the USA in \(2005\) was \(7\) cents per minute. Find the approximate percent decrease in the cost per minute of a long-distance call.

费用下降了 \(41-7=34\) 美分每分钟。

百分比下降为 \(100\cdot\dfrac{34}{41}\%\)，略大于 \(80\%\)，所以最接近的答案是 \(80\)。

所以正确答案是 E。

English

The decrease in cost is \(41-7=34\) cents per minute.

The percent decrease is \(100\cdot\dfrac{34}{41}\%\), which is a little more than \(80\%\), so the closest answer is \(80\).

Thus, E is the correct answer.

7单选

一个房间里 \(5\) 个人的平均年龄是 \(30\) 岁。一名 \(18\) 岁的人离开房间。剩下四个人的平均年龄是多少？

English（选项同上方）

The average age of \(5\) people in a room is \(30\) years. An \(18\)-year-old person leaves the room. What is the average age of the four remaining people?

起初，房间里所有人的总年龄为 \(5 \cdot 30 = 150\) 岁。

这个人离开后，总年龄为 \(150 - 18 = 132\)。剩下 \(4\) 人，平均年龄为 \(\dfrac{132}{4} = 33\)。

所以正确答案是 D。

English

Initially, the total age of everyone in the room is \(5 \cdot 30 = 150\) years.

After the person leaves, the total age is \(150 - 18 = 132.\) With \(4\) people remaining, the average age becomes \(\dfrac{132}{4} = 33.\)

Thus, D is the correct answer.

8单选

在梯形 \(ABCD\) 中，\(\overline{AD}\) 垂直于 \(\overline{DC}\)，\(AD = AB = 3\)，且 \(DC = 6\)。此外，\(E\) 在 \(\overline{DC}\) 上，且 \(\overline{BE}\) 平行于 \(\overline{AD}\)。求 \(\triangle BEC\) 的面积。

English（选项同上方）

In trapezoid \(ABCD,\) \(\overline{AD}\) is perpendicular to \(\overline{DC},\) \(AD = AB = 3,\) and \(DC = 6.\) In addition, \(E\) is on \(\overline{DC},\) and \(\overline{BE}\) is parallel to \(\overline{AD}.\) Find the area of \(\triangle BEC.\)

我们有 \[ EC = DC - DE\] \[ = 6 - 3 \] \[= 3\text{。}\]还知道 \[ BE = AD = 3\text{。}\]因此 \(\triangle BEC\) 的面积为 \[ \dfrac{1}{2} \cdot 3 \cdot 3 = \dfrac{9}{2}\text{。}\]

所以正确答案是 B。

English

We know that \[ EC = DC - DE\]\[ = 6 - 3 \]\[= 3. \] We also know that \[ BE = AD = 3. \] Therefore, the area of \(\triangle BEC\) is \[ \dfrac{1}{2} \cdot 3 \cdot 3 = \dfrac{9}{2}. \]

Thus, B is the correct answer.

9单选

要完成下面的方格，每一行和每一列都必须各出现一次数字 \(1\) 到 \(4\)。右下角方格中会是什么数？

English（选项同上方）

To complete the grid below, each of the digits \(1\) through \(4\) must occur once in each row and once in each column. What number will occupy the lower right-hand square?

第二行最后一个格子必须是 \(1\)，因为该行已有 \(2\) 和 \(3\)，最后一列已有 \(4\)。

接着右上角格子必须是 \(3\)，因为第一行已有 \(1\) 和 \(2\)，最后一列已有 \(1\) 和 \(4\)。

这迫使 \(2\) 位于右下角方格。

所以正确答案是 B。

English

The last square in the second row must be \(1\), since that row already contains \(2\) and \(3\), and the last column already contains \(4\).

Then the top-right square must be \(3\), since the top row already contains \(1\) and \(2\), and the last column already contains \(1\) and \(4\).

This forces the \(2\) to be in the bottom-right square.

Thus, B is the correct answer.

10单选

对任意正整数 \(n\)，定义 \(\boxed{n}\) 为 \(n\) 的所有正因数之和。例如，\[ \boxed{6} = 1 + 2 + 3 + 6 = 12\text{。}\]求 \(\boxed{\boxed{11}}\)。

English（选项同上方）

For any positive integer \(n,\) define \(\boxed{n}\) to be the sum of the positive factors of \(n.\) For example, \[ \boxed{6} = 1 + 2 + 3 + 6 = 12. \] Find \(\boxed{\boxed{11}}.\)

首先，由于 \(11\) 是质数，\[ \boxed{11} = 1 + 11 = 12\text{。}\]接着求 \(\boxed{12}\)。\(12\) 的因数为 \[ 1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 12\text{。}\]相加得到 \(\boxed{12} = 28\)。

所以正确答案是 D。

English

First, we find that \[ \boxed{11} = 1 + 11 = 12 \] since \(11\) is prime. Then we need to find \(\boxed{12}.\) The factors of \(12\) are \[ 1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 12. \] Adding these yields \(\boxed{12} = 28.\)

Thus, D is the correct answer.

11单选

瓷片 \(I\)、\(II\)、\(III\) 和 \(IV\) 被平移，使每个瓷片分别与长方形 \(A\)、\(B\)、\(C\) 和 \(D\) 中的一个重合。在最终排列中，任意两个相邻瓷片公共边上的两个数字必须相同。哪个瓷片被平移到长方形 \(C\)？

English（选项同上方）

Tiles \(I,\) \(II,\) \(III\) and \(IV\) are translated so one tile coincides with each of the rectangles \(A,\) \(B,\) \(C\) and \(D.\) In the final arrangement, the two numbers on any side common to two adjacent tiles must be the same. Which of the tiles is translated to Rectangle \(C?\)

只有瓷片 \(III\) 的边上有 \(0\)，所以瓷片 \(III\) 必须放在 \(C\) 或 \(D\)，使这个边可以在外侧。

瓷片 \(III\) 的右边还有 \(5\)，且没有其他瓷片有 \(5\)。因此瓷片 \(III\) 必须放在 \(D\)，让 \(5\) 在外侧。

\(1\) 标在瓷片 \(III\) 的左边，唯一能与之匹配的是瓷片 \(IV\)，所以瓷片 \(IV\) 放在 \(C\)。

所以正确答案是 D。

English

Only Tile \(III\) has a \(0\) on an edge, so Tile \(III\) must be placed in \(C\) or \(D\), where that edge can be on the outside.

Tile \(III\) also has a \(5\) on its right edge, and no other tile has a \(5\). Therefore Tile \(III\) must be placed in \(D\), with its \(5\) on the outside.

The only tile that can match the \(1\) on the left edge of Tile \(III\) is Tile \(IV\), so Tile \(IV\) is placed in \(C\).

Thus, D is the correct answer.

13单选

如维恩图所示，集合 \(A\) 和 \(B\) 的元素个数相同。它们的并集有 \(2007\) 个元素，交集有 \(1001\) 个元素。求集合 \(A\) 的元素个数。

English（选项同上方）

Sets \(A\) and \(B,\) shown in the Venn diagram, have the same number of elements. Their union has \(2007\) elements and their intersection has \(1001\) elements. Find the number of elements in \(A.\)

设 \(x\) 为集合 \(A\) 和 \(B\) 各自的元素个数，并设 \(y\) 为它们交集的元素个数。

条件给出 \[ 2x - y = 2007 \] 且 \[ y = 1001\text{。}\]将 \(y\) 代入第一式得到 \[ \begin{align*} 2x - 1001 &= 2007 \\ 2x &= 3008 \\ x &= 1504 \end{align*}\text{。}\]

14单选

等腰三角形 \(\triangle ABC\) 的底边长为 \(24\)，面积为 \(60\)。其中一条全等边的长度是多少？

English（选项同上方）

The base of isosceles \(\triangle ABC\) is \(24\) and its area is \(60.\) What is the length of one of the congruent sides?

作 \(\overline{BD}\) 为从 \(B\) 到 \(\overline{AC}\) 的高。

则 \[ 60 = \dfrac{1}{2} \cdot BD \cdot 24\text{，}\]得到 \(BD = 5\)。

在 \(\triangle ABD\) 中使用勾股定理：\[ AB^2 = 5^2 + 12^2 = 169 = 13^2\text{。}\]因此 \(AB = 13\)。

所以正确答案是 C。

English

Construct \(\overline{BD}\) as the altitude from \(B\) to \(\overline{AC}.\)

Then \[ 60 = \dfrac{1}{2} \cdot BD \cdot 24, \] which gives us that \(BD = 5.\)

From this, we apply the Pythagorean Theorem on \(\triangle ABD:\) \[ AB^2 = 5^2 + 12^2 = 169 = 13^2. \] This gives us that \(AB = 13.\)

Thus, C is the correct answer.

15单选

设 \(a\)，\(b\) 和 \(c\) 是满足 \(0 \lt a \lt b \lt c\) 的数。下列哪一项不可能？

English（选项同上方）

Let \(a,\) \(b\) and \(c\) be numbers with \(0 \lt a \lt b \lt c.\) Which of the following is impossible?

已知 \(b \lt c\) 且 \(0 \lt a\)。把这两个不等式相加得到 \[ b \lt c + a\text{。}\]这说明 A 不可能，因此就是正确答案。

为确认这一点，可以给出其他选项可行的例子。

B 和 C：取 \(a = 1\)、\(b = 2\)、\(c = 4\)。

D：取 \(a = \dfrac{1}{3}\)、\(b = \dfrac{1}{2}\)、\(c = 1\)。

E：取 \(a = \dfrac{1}{2}\)、\(b = 1\)、\(c = 2\)。

所以正确答案是 A。

English

We know that \(b \lt c\) and \(0 \lt a.\) Adding these two inequalities together yields \[ b \lt c + a. \] This shows that A is impossible, and therefore the right answer.

To ensure that this is correct, we can show that the other options are possible.

B and C : \(a = 1,\) \(b = 2,\) and \(c = 4\)

D : \(a = \dfrac{1}{3},\) \(b = \dfrac{1}{2},\) and \(c = 1\)

E : \(a = \dfrac{1}{2},\) \(b = 1,\) and \(c = 2\)

Thus, A is the correct answer.

17单选

\(30\) 升油漆混合物中有 \(25\%\) 红色颜料、\(30\%\) 黄色颜料和 \(45\%\) 水。向原混合物中加入五升黄色颜料。新混合物中黄色颜料占百分之多少？

English（选项同上方）

A mixture of \(30\) liters of paint is \(25\%\) red tint, \(30\%\) yellow tint and \(45\%\) water. Five liters of yellow tint are added to the original mixture. What is the percent of yellow tint in the new mixture?

原混合物中黄色颜料为 \(0.3 \times 30 = 9\) 升。加入 \(5\) 升后，黄色颜料共有 \(14\) 升。

新混合物总量为 \(35\) 升，所以黄色颜料百分比为 \[ 100 \cdot \dfrac{14}{35} = 100 \cdot \dfrac{2}{5} = 40 \%\text{。}\]

所以正确答案是 C。

English

The amount of yellow tint in the original mixture is \(0.3 \times 30 = 9\) liters. Adding \(5\) liters results in a total of \(14\) liters of yellow tint.

The new mixture has a total of \(35\) liters, so the percent of yellow tint is \[ 100 \cdot \dfrac{14}{35} = 100 \cdot \dfrac{2}{5} = 40 \%. \]

Thus, C is the correct answer.

18单选

两个 \(99\) 位数 \[303,030,303,\ldots,030,303\] 和 \[505,050,505,\ldots,050,505\] 的乘积，其千位数字为 \(A\)，个位数字为 \(B\)。\(A+B\) 是多少？

English（选项同上方）

The product of the two \(99\)-digit numbers \[303,030,303,\ldots,030,303\] and \[505,050,505,\ldots,050,505\] has thousands digit \(A\) and units digit \(B.\) What is \(A+B?\)

只有最后四位会影响乘积的千位和个位。这两个数分别以 \(0303\) 和 \(0505\) 结尾，所以只需计算 \(303\cdot 505\)。

因为 \(303\cdot 505=153015\)，完整乘积的最后四位是 \(3015\)。

因此 \(A=3\)，\(B=5\)，所以 \(A+B=8\)。

所以正确答案是 D。

English

Only the last four digits can affect the thousands digit and units digit of the product. The two numbers end in \(0303\) and \(0505\), so it is enough to compute \(303\cdot 505\).

Since \(303\cdot 505=153015\), the last four digits of the full product are \(3015\).

This gives \(A=3\) and \(B=5\), so \(A+B=8\).

Thus, D is the correct answer.

19单选

选择两个连续正整数，它们的和小于 \(100\)。把这两个整数分别平方，然后求平方差。下列哪一个可能是这个差？

English（选项同上方）

Pick two consecutive positive integers whose sum is less than \(100.\) Square both of those integers and then find the difference of the squares. Which of the following could be the difference?

设连续正整数为 \(x\) 和 \(x+1\)。它们的和为 \(2x+1\)，小于 \(100\)。

它们的平方差为 \[ \begin{gathered} (x+1)^2-x^2 \\ = (x+1+x)(x+1-x) \\ = 2x+1 \end{gathered}\text{。}\]

所以这个差必须是小于 \(100\) 的奇数。选项中只有 \(79\) 可能，它由 \(39\) 和 \(40\) 得到。

所以正确答案是 C。

English

Let the consecutive positive integers be \(x\) and \(x+1\). Their sum is \(2x+1\), which is less than \(100\).

The difference of their squares is \[ \begin{gathered} (x+1)^2-x^2 \\ = (x+1+x)(x+1-x) \\ = 2x+1. \end{gathered} \]

So the difference must be an odd number less than \(100\). Among the choices, \(79\) is the only possibility, and it occurs for \(39\) and \(40\).

Thus, C is the correct answer.

20单选

在分区赛前，Unicorns 赢了他们篮球比赛的 \(45 \%\)。分区赛中，他们又赢六场、输两场，最终以胜率一半结束赛季。Unicorns 总共打了多少场比赛？

English（选项同上方）

Before district play, the Unicorns had won \(45 \%\) of their basketball games. During district play, they won six more games and lost two, to finish the season having won half their games. How many games did the Unicorns play in all?

设 Unicorns 分区赛前打了 \(x\) 场比赛。那么分区赛前赢了 \(0.45x\) 场，分区赛后共赢了 \(0.45x+6\) 场。

他们总共打了 \(x+8\) 场。题意给出 \[0.45x+6=\dfrac{x+8}{2}\text{。}\]

解得 \(0.45x+6=0.5x+4\)，所以 \(2=0.05x\)，\(x=40\)。因此 Unicorns 总共打了 \(40+8=48\) 场。

所以正确答案是 A。

English

Let \(x\) be the number of games the Unicorns had played before district play. Then they had won \(0.45x\) games before district play and \(0.45x+6\) games after district play.

In total, they played \(x+8\) games. The problem statement tells us that \[0.45x+6=\dfrac{x+8}{2}.\]

Solving gives \(0.45x+6=0.5x+4\), so \(2=0.05x\), and \(x=40\). Therefore, the Unicorns played \(40+8=48\) games in all.

Thus, A is the correct answer.

21单选

从一副牌中发出两张牌。这副牌有四张红牌，分别标为 \(A\)、\(B\)、\(C\)、\(D\)，还有四张绿牌，也分别标为 \(A\)、\(B\)、\(C\)、\(D\)。一对获胜牌是两张同色牌或两张同字母牌。抽到一对获胜牌的概率是多少？

English（选项同上方）

Two cards are dealt from a deck of four red cards labeled \(A,\) \(B,\) \(C,\) \(D\) and four green cards labeled \(A,\) \(B,\) \(C,\) \(D.\) A winning pair is two of the same color or two of the same letter. What is the probability of drawing a winning pair?

抽出第一张牌后，还剩 \(7\) 张。其中 \(3\) 张同色，\(1\) 张同字母。因此会与第一张牌形成获胜牌对的有 \(4\) 张，它们都在剩余的 \(7\) 张牌中。

概率为 \(\dfrac{4}{7}\)。

所以正确答案是 D。

English

After drawing the first card, there are \(7\) left. \(3\) of them have the same color, and \(1\) of them has the same letter. Therefore, there are \(4\) out of \(7\) possibilities that result in a winning pair.

The probability of drawing a pair is then \(\dfrac{4}{7}.\)

Thus, D is the correct answer.

22单选

一只旅鼠坐在边长为 \(10\) 米的正方形的一个角上。它沿对角线朝对面的顶点跑了 \(6.2\) 米。它停下，向右转 \(90^{\circ}\)，又跑了 \(2\) 米。一位科学家测量旅鼠到正方形每条边的最短距离。这四个距离的平均数是多少米？

English（选项同上方）

A lemming sits at a corner of a square with side length \(10\) meters. The lemming runs \(6.2\) meters along a diagonal toward the opposite corner. It stops, makes a \(90^{\circ}\) right turn and runs \(2\) more meters. A scientist measures the shortest distance between the lemming and each side of the square. What is the average of these four distances in meters?

根据题目给出的长度，旅鼠停下后仍在正方形内。

因为旅鼠仍在正方形内，它到两条水平边的距离和为 \(10\) 米，到两条竖直边的距离和也一样。因此 \(4\) 个距离之和为 \(20\) 米，平均数为 \(20 \div 4 = 5\) 米。

所以正确答案是 C。

English

Based on the lengths given in the problem, the lemming is still in the square after it stops.

Since the lemming is still in the square, the sum of the distances to the horizontal sides is \(10\) meters and the same for the vertical sides. Therefore, the \(4\) distances sum to \(20\) meters, making the average \(20 \div 4 = 5\) meters.

Thus, C is the correct answer.

23单选

图中 \(5 \times 5\) 方格里的阴影风车形面积是多少？

English（选项同上方）

What is the area of the shaded pinwheel shown in the \(5 \times 5\) grid?

可以用总面积减去未阴影面积来求阴影面积。

有 \(4\) 个边长为 \(1\) 的正方形，每个面积为 \(1\)，对未阴影面积贡献共 \(4\)。

还有 \(4\) 个底为 \(3\)、高为 \(\dfrac{5}{2}\) 的三角形，总面积为 \[ 4 \cdot \dfrac{1}{2} \cdot 3 \cdot \dfrac{5}{2} = 15\text{。}\]

未阴影总面积为 \(4 + 15 = 19\)。

整个方格面积为 \(5^2 = 25\)，所以阴影面积为 \(25 - 19 = 6\)。

所以正确答案是 B。

English

We can find the area of the shaded part by subtracting the area of the unshaded part from the whole area.

There are \(4\) squares with side length \(1,\) contributing \(1\) each to the unshaded area, for a total of \(4.\)

There are also \(4\) triangles with base \(3\) and height \(\dfrac{5}{2}.\) This contributes a total area of \[ 4 \cdot \dfrac{1}{2} \cdot 3 \cdot \dfrac{5}{2} = 15. \]

The total unshaded area is therefore \(4 + 15 = 19.\)

The total area is \(5^2 = 25,\) and subtracting the unshaded area yields \(25 - 19 = 6\) as the area of the shaded region.

Thus, B is the correct answer.

24单选

一个袋子里有四张纸片，分别标有数字 \(1\)、\(2\)、\(3\)、\(4\)，没有重复。从中不放回地一次抽出三张，用来组成一个三位数。这个三位数是 \(3\) 的倍数的概率是多少？

English（选项同上方）

A bag contains four pieces of paper, each labeled with one of the digits \(1,\) \(2,\) \(3\) or \(4,\) with no repeats. Three of these pieces are drawn, one at a time without replacement, to construct a three-digit number. What is the probability that the three-digit number is a multiple of \(3?\)

一个数能被 \(3\) 整除，当且仅当它的数字和能被 \(3\) 整除。

从 \(1,2,3,4\) 中取三个不同数字，数字和能被 \(3\) 整除的只有 \((1,2,3)\) 和 \((2,3,4)\)。

因此所组成的数是 \(3\) 的倍数，当且仅当袋中剩下的是 \(1\) 或 \(4\)。

每种事件概率都是 \(\dfrac{1}{4}\)，总概率为 \(2\cdot\dfrac{1}{4}=\dfrac{1}{2}\)。

所以正确答案是 C。

English

Recall that a number is divisible by \(3\) if the sum of its digits is divisible by \(3\).

The only triples of distinct digits from \(1,2,3,4\) whose sum is divisible by \(3\) are \((1,2,3)\) and \((2,3,4)\).

This means the constructed number is a multiple of \(3\) exactly when either \(1\) or \(4\) is left in the bag.

Each of these events has probability \(\dfrac{1}{4}\), for a total probability of \(2\cdot\dfrac{1}{4}=\dfrac{1}{2}\).

Thus, C is the correct answer.

25单选

下图的飞镖盘中，外圆半径为 \(6\)，内圆半径为 \(3\)。三条半径把每个圆分成三个全等区域，并标出分值。飞镖击中某区域的概率与该区域面积成正比。当两支飞镖击中这个靶时，得分为击中区域分值之和。得分为奇数的概率是多少？

English（选项同上方）

On the dart board shown in the figure below, the outer circle has radius \(6\) and the inner circle has radius \(3.\) Three radii divide each circle into three congruent regions, with point values shown. The probability that a dart will hit a given region is proportional to the area of the region. When two darts hit this board, the score is the sum of the point values of the regions hit. What is the probability that the score is odd?

外圆面积为 \(6^2\pi = 36\pi\)，内圆面积为 \(3^2\pi = 9\pi\)。因此外环面积为 \(36\pi - 9\pi = 27\pi\)。

所以击中一个外环扇区的概率为 \(\dfrac{9\pi}{36\pi} = \dfrac{1}{4}\)。类似地，击中一个内圆扇区的概率为 \(\dfrac{3\pi}{36\pi} = \dfrac{1}{12}\)。

汇总所有可能，击中 \(1\) 分区域的概率为 \[ \dfrac{1}{12} + 2 \cdot \dfrac{1}{4} = \dfrac{7}{12}\text{。}\]击中 \(2\) 分区域的概率为 \[ 2 \cdot \dfrac{1}{12} + \dfrac{1}{4} = \dfrac{5}{12}\text{。}\]

得到奇数得分的唯一方式是一支击中 \(1\) 分，另一支击中 \(2\) 分。

两种顺序的总概率为 \[ 2 \cdot \dfrac{5}{12} \cdot \dfrac{7}{12} = \dfrac{35}{72}\text{。}\]

所以正确答案是 B。

English

The area of the outer circle is \(6^2\pi = 36\pi,\) and the area of the inner circle is \(3^2\pi = 9\pi.\) Therefore, the area of the outer ring is \(36\pi - 9\pi = 27\pi.\)

This means that the probability of hitting an outer segment is \(\dfrac{9\pi}{36\pi} = \dfrac{1}{4}.\) Similarly, the probability of hitting an inner segment is \(\dfrac{3\pi}{36\pi} = \dfrac{1}{12}.\)

Summing over all possibilities, the probability of hitting a \(1\) is \[ \dfrac{1}{12} + 2 \cdot \dfrac{1}{4} = \dfrac{7}{12}. \] Similarly, the probability of hitting a \(2\) is \[ 2 \cdot \dfrac{1}{12} + \dfrac{1}{4} = \dfrac{5}{12}. \]

The only way to get an odd score is to hit one \(1\) and one \(2.\)

The probability of hitting these numbers in either order is \[ 2 \cdot \dfrac{5}{12} \cdot \dfrac{7}{12} = \dfrac{35}{72}. \]

Thus, B is the correct answer.
