\(M\) 比 \(N\) 大的百分率为：

English（选项同上方）

The percent that \(M\) is greater than \(N\) is:

从 \(N\) 增加到 \(M\) 的增加量为 \(M-N\)。除以原数量并乘以 \(100\)，得到 \[ \frac{100(M-N)}{N}\text{。}\]

因此，正确答案是 B。

English

The increase from \(N\) to \(M\) is \(M-N.\) Dividing by the original quantity and multiplying by \(100\) gives \[ \frac{100(M-N)}{N}. \]

Thus, the correct answer is B.

一块长方形田地的宽是长的一半，四周共用 \(x\) 码栅栏围住。用 \(x\) 表示其面积为：

English（选项同上方）

A rectangular field is half as wide as it is long and is completely enclosed by \(x\) yards of fencing. The area in terms of \(x\) is:

设宽为 \(w\)，则长为 \(2w\)。由栅栏总长可得 \(2(w+2w)=6w=x\)，所以 \(w=\frac{x}{6}\)。因此， \[ A=w(2w)=2\left(\frac{x}{6}\right)^2=\frac{x^2}{18}\text{。}\]

因此，正确答案是 D。

English

Let the width be \(w\), so the length is \(2w.\) The fencing gives \(2(w+2w)=6w=x,\) hence \(w=\frac{x}{6}.\) Therefore \[ A=w(2w)=2\left(\frac{x}{6}\right)^2=\frac{x^2}{18}. \]

Thus, the correct answer is D.

3单选

English（选项同上方）

If the length of a diagonal of a square is \(a+b,\) then the area of the square is:

若边长为 \(s\)，对角线 \(d=a+b\)，则 \(d=s\sqrt2\)。因此， \[ s^2=\frac{d^2}{2}=\frac12(a+b)^2\text{。}\]

因此，正确答案是 B。

English

If \(s\) is the side length and \(d=a+b\) is the diagonal, then \(d=s\sqrt2.\) Thus \[ s^2=\frac{d^2}{2}=\frac12(a+b)^2. \]

Thus, the correct answer is B.

4单选

一座平顶长方体谷仓宽 \(10\) 码、长 \(13\) 码、高 \(5\) 码。谷仓内外墙面和天花板都要粉刷，但屋顶和地面不刷。需要粉刷的总面积为多少平方码？

English（选项同上方）

A barn with a flat roof is rectangular in shape, \(10\) yd. wide, \(13\) yd. long, and \(5\) yd. high. It is to be painted inside and outside, and on the ceiling, but not on the roof or floor. The total number of square yards to be painted is:

四面墙单侧的面积为 \[ 2(13\cdot5)+2(10\cdot5)=230\text{。}\]内外两侧共需粉刷 \(2(230)=460\)。天花板再增加 \(10\cdot13=130\)，所以总面积为 \(460+130=590\)。

因此，正确答案是 D。

English

The four walls have one-sided area \[ 2(13\cdot5)+2(10\cdot5)=230. \] Painting them inside and outside contributes \(2(230)=460.\) The ceiling adds \(10\cdot13=130,\) so the total is \(460+130=590.\)

Thus, the correct answer is D.

第一次售价为 \(10{,}000(1.10)=11{,}000\)。卖回时的售价为 \(11{,}000(0.90)=9{,}900\)。\(A\) 先生收到 \(11{,}000\)，再付出 \(9{,}900\)，所以获利 \(1{,}100\)。

English（选项同上方）

The bottom, side, and front areas of a rectangular box are known. The product of these areas is equal to:

若三条棱长为 \(l,w,h\)，则三个面的面积为 \(lw\)、\(wh\) 和 \(hl\)。它们的乘积为 \[ (lw)(wh)(hl)=l^2w^2h^2=(lwh)^2\text{，}\]即体积的平方。

English

Thus, the correct answer is D.

9单选

先画一个边长为 \(a\) 的等边三角形，再连接其三边中点，得到一个新的等边三角形；随后连接第二个三角形的三边中点，得到第三个等边三角形；如此无限继续。所有这些三角形周长之和的极限为：

English（选项同上方）

An equilateral triangle is drawn with a side of length \(a.\) A new equilateral triangle is formed by joining the midpoints of the sides of the first one. Then a third equilateral triangle is formed by joining the midpoints of the sides of the second; and so on forever. The limit of the sum of the perimeters of all the triangles thus drawn is:

第一个三角形的周长为 \(3a\)，以后每个周长都是前一个的一半。因此，总和为 \[ \begin{aligned} &3a\left(1+\frac12+\frac14+\cdots\right)\\ &\quad=3a\cdot\frac{1}{1-\frac12}\\ &\quad=6a \end{aligned}\text{。}\]

因此，正确答案是 D。

English

The first perimeter is \(3a,\) and every later perimeter is half the preceding one. Hence the total is \[ \begin{aligned} &3a\left(1+\frac12+\frac14+\cdots\right)\\ &\quad=3a\cdot\frac{1}{1-\frac12}\\ &\quad=6a. \end{aligned} \]

Thus, the correct answer is D.

The limit of the sum of an infinite number of terms in a geometric progression is \(\dfrac{a}{1-r},\) where \(a\) denotes the first term and \(-1\lt r\lt1\) denotes the common ratio. The limit of the sum of their squares is:

各项平方后仍组成等比数列，首项为 \(a^2\)，公比为 \(r^2\)。因为 \(|r|\lt1\)，所以其和为 \[ \frac{a^2}{1-r^2}\text{。}\]

15单选

对于 \(n\) 的一切整数值，表达式 \(n^3-n\) 恒能被下列哪个最大的数整除？

English（选项同上方）

The largest number by which the expression \(n^3-n\) is divisible for all possible integral values of \(n\) is:

有 \[ n^3-n=n(n-1)(n+1)\text{，}\]这是三个连续整数的乘积。其中一个能被 \(3\) 整除，且至少一个是偶数，所以该乘积恒能被 \(6\) 整除。取 \(n=2\) 时，表达式的值恰为 \(6\)，所以不存在更大的整数能恒整除该表达式。

因此，正确答案是 E。

English

We have \[ n^3-n=n(n-1)(n+1), \] a product of three consecutive integers. One is divisible by \(3\) and at least one is even, so the product is always divisible by \(6.\) Taking \(n=2\) gives exactly \(6,\) so no larger integer always divides it.

Thus, the correct answer is E.

18单选

要将表达式 \(21x^2+ax+21\) 分解为两个系数为整数的线性不可约二项式之积。当 \(a\) 是下列哪一项时，可以完成这种分解？

English（选项同上方）

The expression \(21x^2+ax+21\) is to be factored into two linear prime binomial factors with integer coefficients. This can be done if \(a\) is:

设 \[ \begin{aligned} &21x^2+ax+21\\ &\quad=(Ax+B)(Cx+D) \end{aligned}\text{。}\]则 \(AC=BD=21\)，所以 \(A,B,C,D\) 均为奇数。因此，\(a=AD+BC\) 为偶数。某些偶数确实可行；例如，\[ \begin{aligned} &(3x+7)(7x+3)\\ &\quad=21x^2+58x+21 \end{aligned}\text{。}\]但并非每个偶数都可行。

因此，正确答案是 D。

English

Suppose \[ \begin{aligned} &21x^2+ax+21\\ &\quad=(Ax+B)(Cx+D). \end{aligned} \] Then \(AC=BD=21,\) so \(A,B,C,D\) are odd. Therefore \(a=AD+BC\) is even. Some even values work; for example, \[ \begin{aligned} &(3x+7)(7x+3)\\ &\quad=21x^2+58x+21. \end{aligned} \] Not every even value works.

Thus, the correct answer is D.

25单选

一个正方形的面积数值等于其周长数值；一个等边三角形的面积数值也等于其周长数值。比较它们的边心距，前者的边心距：

English（选项同上方）

The apothem of a square having its area numerically equal to its perimeter is compared with the apothem of an equilateral triangle having its area numerically equal to its perimeter. The first apothem will be:

边心距为 \(r\)、周长为 \(P\) 的任意正多边形，其面积为 \(A=\frac12rP\)。若面积数值等于非零的周长数值，则 \[ \frac12rP=P\text{，}\]所以 \(r=2\)。正方形与等边三角形都满足这一结论，因此二者的边心距相等。

English

Every regular polygon with apothem \(r\) and perimeter \(P\) has area \(A=\frac12rP.\) If its numerical area equals its nonzero perimeter, then \[ \frac12rP=P, \] so \(r=2.\) This applies to both the square and the equilateral triangle, so their apothems are equal.

Thus, the correct answer is A.

设两条直角边为 \(a,b\)。从其相对的锐角顶点所作中线的长度平方满足 \[ a^2+\frac{b^2}{4}=25,\qquad \frac{a^2}{4}+b^2=40\text{。}\]解得 \(a^2=16\) 和 \(b^2=36\)。因此，斜边 \(c\) 满足 \[ c^2=a^2+b^2=52\text{，}\]所以 \(c=2\sqrt{13}\)。

因此，正确答案是 D。

English

Let the legs be \(a,b.\) The medians from their opposite acute vertices have squared lengths \[ a^2+\frac{b^2}{4}=25,\qquad \frac{a^2}{4}+b^2=40. \] Solving gives \(a^2=16\) and \(b^2=36.\) Thus the hypotenuse \(c\) satisfies \[ c^2=a^2+b^2=52, \] so \(c=2\sqrt{13}.\)

Thus, the correct answer is D.

汤姆、迪克和哈里一起开始一段 \(100\) 英里的旅程。汤姆与哈里乘汽车，以每小时 \(25\) 英里的速度前进；迪克则以每小时 \(5\) 英里的速度步行。行驶一段距离后，哈里下车，改以每小时 \(5\) 英里的速度继续步行；汤姆掉头去接迪克，并使迪克与哈里同时到达终点。整个旅程所需的小时数为：

English（选项同上方）

Tom, Dick, and Harry started out on a \(100\)-mile journey. Tom and Harry went by automobile at the rate of \(25\) mph, while Dick walked at the rate of \(5\) mph. After a certain distance, Harry got off and walked on at \(5\) mph, while Tom went back for Dick and got him to the destination at the same time that Harry arrived. The number of hours required for the trip was:

令 \(t_1,t_2,t_3\) 分别为哈里下车前汽车行驶的时间、汽车返回接迪克的时间以及汽车载着迪克向前行驶的时间。汽车、迪克和哈里各自行进 \(100\) 英里，因此 \[ \begin{aligned} 25t_1-25t_2+25t_3&=100,\\ 5t_1+5t_2+25t_3&=100,\\ 25t_1+5t_2+5t_3&=100 \end{aligned}\text{。}\]各式除以 \(5\) 并求解，得到 \(t_1=3,\ t_2=2,\ t_3=3\)。因此，他们共同的行程时间为 \(t_1+t_2+t_3=8\) 小时。

因此，正确答案是 D。

English

Let \(t_1,t_2,t_3\) be the car’s times before Harry leaves it, while it returns for Dick, and while it carries Dick forward. The car, Dick, and Harry each cover \(100\) miles, giving \[ \begin{aligned} 25t_1-25t_2+25t_3&=100,\\ 5t_1+5t_2+25t_3&=100,\\ 25t_1+5t_2+5t_3&=100. \end{aligned} \] Dividing by \(5\) and solving yields \(t_1=3,\ t_2=2,\ t_3=3.\) Therefore the common travel time is \(t_1+t_2+t_3=8\) hours.

Thus, the correct answer is D.
